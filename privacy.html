Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit call-leary-plumbing.com or telephone the number shown on this independent plumbing information and referral website.

1. Introduction

This website provides information about plumbing services and encourages visitors to make telephone enquiries. It is independently operated and does not necessarily provide plumbing work directly. This policy describes information that may be collected, used or shared in connection with the website and telephone enquiries.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with the website or its telephone service, information handled may include:

  • Details you choose to provide during an enquiry, such as your name, location and the type of plumbing assistance requested.
  • Your telephone number, caller identification and basic call information, where available.
  • Information you share during a telephone conversation to help explain your request.
  • Basic technical information made available through ordinary website requests, such as IP address, browser type, device details and pages requested.

The current homepage does not contain a contact submission form. This policy does not imply that you must provide personal information to browse the website.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, help connect callers with relevant independent plumbing service providers, facilitate consultations or services you request, maintain and improve the website and enquiry process, prevent misuse, and meet applicable legal obligations.

4. Lead Generation and Third-Party Service Providers

This website operates as an independent information and lead-generation service. Telephone enquiries may be handled, transferred or referred through third-party call-routing and referral networks to independent businesses offering relevant plumbing services.

The website operator may receive compensation for qualifying telephone enquiries or referrals.

Information provided during an enquiry, including relevant caller details, may be shared with telecommunications providers, referral networks and independent service providers for the purposes of handling, routing and responding to enquiries. Independent service providers are responsible for their own services, pricing, appointments and business practices. A telephone enquiry does not, by itself, mean you have agreed to unrelated marketing calls or messages.

5. Call Tracking and Recording

Telephone enquiries may be routed and tracked using third-party telecommunications and referral services. Information collected may include the caller's telephone number, call date, time, duration, routing information and call outcome.

Calls may be connected to independent third-party service providers. Voicemail messages left through this website's telephone system are recorded and may be reviewed or shared with relevant referral partners or service providers to assist with handling enquiries. Please do not include payment card information or other sensitive details in a voicemail.

Telephone conversations handled by participating service providers or referral networks may also be recorded, subject to applicable laws and any required notification or consent. This does not mean every telephone conversation is recorded by this website.

Call recordings and associated information may be retained for enquiry handling, service administration, quality assurance, fraud prevention and other legitimate business purposes, subject to applicable legal requirements. If call-handling or recording practices change, this policy will be updated as appropriate.

6. Cookies and Website Analytics

The homepage supplied for this website does not include an identified analytics service, advertising pixel or custom cookie-setting script. Ordinary hosting systems may still process technical request information needed to deliver and protect the website. If cookies, analytics or advertising tracking are added later, this policy and any required visitor notices should be updated before use.

7. Information Sharing

Information relevant to an enquiry may be shared with independent service providers who answer or receive calls, and with telecommunications, website hosting or other technical suppliers that help operate the website and its telephone service. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law. This policy does not suggest that enquiry information is kept solely by the website operator.

8. Data Security and Retention

Reasonable safeguards should be used to protect information handled in connection with this website. No internet or telephone service can guarantee complete security. Information should be kept only for as long as reasonably needed for the relevant enquiry, business administration, dispute handling or applicable legal requirements. Specific retention periods have not yet been confirmed.

9. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on where you live and the laws that apply, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of certain personal information, or to object to particular uses. Requests must be directed to the organisation that actually holds the relevant information, which may be the website operator, telecommunications provider or service provider that handled your call.

For privacy questions or requests, contact the independent website operator at dean AT djfpublishing DOT com (replace AT with @ and DOT with .). Please include the website address in your message so we can identify the enquiry.

10. Third-Party Websites and Services

If you interact with an independent plumbing business or visit an external website, that business or site may handle your information under its own privacy policy. Please review the relevant provider's terms and privacy information when appropriate.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the website, call-routing arrangements or information-handling practices change. Any revised version will be published on this page.

This is an independent plumbing information and lead-generation website and is not affiliated with the former Leary Plumbing business. It does not represent that the former business is operating or that the website operator performs plumbing work.
